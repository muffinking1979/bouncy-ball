Bouncy Ball

Bounce over the pits, through the cave, and out to the gold flag at the far end.

  • The ball bounces on its own. Steer it with ← →.
  • Hold ↑ as you land for a big bounce.
  • Hold ↓ to drop faster and land with a small hop. You'll need it where the cave roof is low.
  • Slopes kick the ball the way they lean.
  • You have 3 balls. Lose one and you go back to the start, or to the red flag once you've reached it. Lose all 3 and you start over from the beginning.

Space continues, R starts over, M mutes
